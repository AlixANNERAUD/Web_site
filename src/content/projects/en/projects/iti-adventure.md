# ITI Adventure

**Java** · 2024

> A text-based Java RPG exploring object-oriented design and software patterns.

## Overview

A text-based RPG developed in Java, applying OOP principles through inheritance hierarchies for characters and items, a modular event system, and design patterns such as Strategy and Observer.

## Engineering approach

The educational value is the decomposition of a text RPG into objects, events and responsibilities. Strategy and Observer patterns make the rules extensible without a single monolithic class.

## Scope and limitations

Academic or research work, potentially collaborative. It illustrates an engineering method or experiment rather than claiming a commercially deployed product.
