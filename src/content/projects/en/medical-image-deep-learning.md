# Medical image segmentation

**Python · Deep Learning · nnU-Net · CellViT** · Academic project

> Histopathology tissue and cell segmentation/classification experiments using nnU-Net, CellViT++ and HoVer-Net.

## Overview

An academic histopathology image segmentation and cell classification project. The repository includes data preparation and training scripts for nnU-Net, CellViT++, and HoVer-Net, including SLURM-based workflows. The work focuses on integrating and experimenting with established deep-learning architectures rather than claiming a new model designed from scratch. [Source and workflow](https://github.com/AlixANNERAUD/Deep_learning_project).

## Engineering approach

The workflow prepares histopathology images and experiments with existing segmentation and classification architectures through training scripts. It is integration and evaluation work, not a claim to have invented nnU-Net or CellViT.

## Scope and limitations

Academic or research work, potentially collaborative. It illustrates an engineering method or experiment rather than claiming a commercially deployed product.
