# INSAtram

**Free pascal** · 2022

> A Free Pascal/SDL tram network management and passenger-flow simulation game.

## Overview

A tram network management game built in Free Pascal with SDL 1.2, featuring route planning, passenger flow simulation, and resource management.

## Engineering approach

The simulator models a transport network, route planning and passenger movement. Built in Free Pascal with SDL, it illustrates how to structure an interactive simulation.

## Scope and limitations

Academic or research work, potentially collaborative. It illustrates an engineering method or experiment rather than claiming a commercially deployed product.
